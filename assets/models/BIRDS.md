# Bird model attribution

All five original textured GLBs are by **Poly by Google**, under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The full license is in `source/birds/CC-BY-3.0.txt`; source hashes are in `source/birds/manifest.json`.

- **seagull**: [Flying seagull](https://poly.pizza/m/6Tpj_vcWP3f). Normalized source textured body; procedural eight-pose wing flapping and rolled death pose.
- **crow**: [Crow](https://poly.pizza/m/1MIvWQ5Q3R9). Normalized source textured body; procedural eight-pose wing flapping and rolled death pose. Perched body tilted forward with added feather-shaped flight wings sampling the original texture.
- **sparrow**: [Sparrow](https://poly.pizza/m/3rTjKefT184). Normalized source textured body; procedural eight-pose wing flapping and rolled death pose. Perched body tilted forward with added feather-shaped flight wings sampling the original texture.
