# Original modern city asset collection

These assets are original project geometry and procedural textures generated by
`tools/build_modern_assets.py`. They use no downloaded models, photographic
textures, vehicle branding, or copied game assets. Existing third-party credits
remain in their own asset records.

## Contents

- Coastal Office and Terrace Apartments: recessed glazing, separate concrete,
  metal and glass, storefront doors/handles, canopy, balcony railings, rooftop
  ventilation, and four authored LOD levels.
- Compact Pistol and Carbine: bevelled metal/polymer surfaces, muzzle and sights,
  grip detail; the carbine has a rail, magazine, stock and optic.
- Street Lamp and Twin Lamp: tapered poles, arms, LED lenses, cooling ribs,
  service panel and anchor bolts.
- Slatted wood bench, fluted street bin, reflective-band bollard, rounded
  bicycle rack, planted stone trough and hydrant.

12 editable GLBs are in `assets/models/source/modern/`. They embed their PBR
maps and preserve material sections. Source coordinates are metres, Y up, with
+Z forward for cars and weapons. Open them in Blender or a compatible editor.
Source files are excluded by the existing game package workflow; they are
included in the separate asset archive.

18 indexed M3D2 meshes and their material sidecars/maps are in
`assets/models/baked/modern/`. `manifest.json` lists geometry counts, bounds,
material names and hashes. Every `.pbr` range refers to its own base-colour,
normal and linear ORM map. The DX11 renderer uses wrap sampling for these
tileable maps; legacy model sampling remains compatible.
All 54 authored maps are 2048×2048. Concrete pores, stone grain, brushed metal,
wood fibres, rubber grain, lens ribs and paint microtexture use periodic fields;
normal and roughness maps follow their surface structure. Glass stays smooth.
`materials.json` records source-map hashes and metre scale. `textures.json`
records the 54 BC7/BC5/BC7 runtime DDS files, full 12-level mip chains, hashes,
formats, source identities and the pinned compressor. Each range also uses
the ORM red channel as occlusion. PNG maps remain available for editing and
embedded GLB import; the game archive includes the DDS files.
Colour mip filtering runs in linear light. Normal mip filtering averages and
renormalizes directions, keeping coherence in ORM alpha for distant roughness
adjustment. BC5 normals reconstruct positive Z in the DX11 shader. The legacy
PNG/JPEG path retains generated mips and its original normal-alpha convention.
Authored meshes opt out of generic displacement/tessellation, and their normal
maps replace the legacy procedural surface overlay.
An optional `SURFACE1 clearcoat clearcoatRoughness glassIor` suffix after the
alpha fields preserves the legacy sidecar reader contract and adds surface
factors to the DX11 reader. Paint and hydrant materials have a separate clear
coat; glazing declares IOR 1.5. The editable GLBs carry corresponding
`KHR_materials_clearcoat` and `KHR_materials_ior` extensions.

## Integration

The first two city parcels and a deterministic subset of other buildings use
the new facade meshes. The procedural Aurora vehicle files and generator
recipes were removed; vehicles now use [downloaded authored models](VEHICLE_EXPANSION.md).
Pistol and rifle gameplay IDs use the new weapon
meshes with the existing hand/muzzle alignment. Original assets remain available
as fallbacks when this collection is absent.

City lamp placements now use the authored single-arm lamp, with LED glow and
local-light positions aligned at night. Deterministic, distance-limited bench,
bin, bicycle-rack, bollard, planter and hydrant placements furnish the sidewalks.
Marina promenade lamps and benches use the twin-arm lamp and wood bench.
All scene meshes use the existing GPU instance path. Moving imported vehicle
parts and held weapons opt out of temporal history.

## Reproduce

```powershell
python -m pip install numpy pillow
./tools/bootstrap_texconv.ps1
python tools/build_modern_assets.py
python tools/cook_modern_textures.py
python tests/modern_assets.py
python tests/modern_texture_quality.py
./build.ps1 -RunTests
./tools/verify_modern_assets.ps1
```

Geometry uses Python's standard library; texture authoring/cooking uses NumPy
and Pillow. The compressor is Microsoft's DirectXTex May 2026 release, verified
against its published SHA256. It is an offline authoring tool, not a runtime
dependency. [Texconv documentation](https://github.com/microsoft/DirectXTex/wiki/Texconv)
describes its BC7/BC5 and DX10 DDS output. The geometry check verifies
indices, finite values, normal lengths, triangle winding, hashes and embedded
GLB structure. The runtime asset suite checks PBR map coverage, the four LOD
chains, transparency bounds, sampler metadata and temporal-history routing.

Studio previews are rendered from the actual editable GLBs using the pinned
Blender 4.5.14 LTS already installed by the source-conversion workflow:

```powershell
$env:BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES = "$PWD/build-tools/blender-user"
./build-tools/blender-4.5.14-windows-x64/blender.exe --background --factory-startup --python tools/render_modern_assets.py
python tools/compose_modern_assets.py
```

Review images and DX11 capture flags/hashes are recorded in
`evidence/modern-assets-20260928/`.

## Scope of this slice

This is an original procedural asset collection. Vehicle wheels/doors remain
static geometry; existing physics and controls are retained. New street
furniture is decorative and has no new collision or destruction behaviour.
Glass uses Fresnel-weighted blended raster shading with environment and direct
light reflections. Painted surfaces use a separate geometric-normal clearcoat
lobe for direct and probe lighting. These are factor-driven layers; coat maps,
refractive transmission and interior parallax remain open. Sun highlights and
depth-occluded solar lens flare are integrated in the DX11 renderer; broader
lighting, material and texture residency requirements remain separate work.
Full mip chains are currently resident together; distance-based mip streaming,
bounded incremental GPU uploads and selective 4K surfaces are still open.
Moving-scene material/LOD tuning and target discrete-GPU performance need
further review. Do not describe this collection as GTA VI asset quality.
