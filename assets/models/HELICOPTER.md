# Helicopter attribution

Helicopter by **Poly by Google**, [source](https://poly.pizza/m/cTzINMr0WdS), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Source GLB, SHA-256, license and modifications are retained in `source/helicopter/`. The DX11 vehicle uses the rigid assemblies from `tools/bake_expansion_models.py`. The source contains colors and sparse UVs, with no image textures. `tools/texture_vehicle_surfaces.py --vehicle helicopter` adds original project-authored paint, opaque cockpit/cabin glazing, door and panel details, fasteners and mechanical surface maps, plus projected UVs. Geometry, rotor pivots and collision extents remain unchanged. `tools/import_helicopter.py` retains the legacy bake.
