DX11 masonry debris and tank aiming (2026-10-04)
===============================================

`masonry.png`: a building blast with settled brick, hollow block, concrete
chunk and exposed-rebar slab meshes. Original runtime geometry is defined in
`src/masonry.h`; collision uses its hull vertices and hollow-block walls.

`tank-aim.png`: tank hull facing +X, with turret following camera yaw 0.9
radians and barrel elevated 0.25 radians. The source tank was rebaked into
separate authored joints without losing triangles.
`tank-aim-side.png` shows those same joints from a side angle after leaving
the tank; its parked turret keeps the previous aim.

Captures were generated by `tools/verify_masonry_tank.ps1` and visually
inspected. `ctest.txt` records the full regression run. The expansion suite
checks independent azimuth/elevation, rolled hull aiming, muzzle positions,
mechanical limits, unoccupied turret retention, all five rubble types,
camera aim retention while driving/steering and synchronization after Jolt,
motion/settling, lifetime expiry, load cleanup and traversal of the hole after
physical rubble clears. OpenGL rendering is unchanged.
